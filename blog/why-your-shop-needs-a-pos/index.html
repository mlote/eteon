Why your shop needs a point of sale system

POS October 20, 2026 MicFi
M

If you run a shop with a notebook, a calculator, and a lot of memory, you already know the pain. Sales get missed, stock counts do not match, and at the end of the month it is hard to say what actually made money.

A point of sale system puts all of that in one place. Every sale is recorded, stock is updated as you sell, and a receipt is printed or sent automatically.

What a POS actually changes

  • Every sale is recorded, so you always know your numbers.
  • Stock updates in real time, so you know what to reorder.
  • Receipts are consistent and professional.
  • Staff can sell faster with less chance of errors.

It pays for itself

Most shops lose more money to untracked sales and stock errors than a POS costs. Once you can see clearly what is selling and what is not, you make better decisions.
